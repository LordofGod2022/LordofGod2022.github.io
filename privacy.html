Privacy policy: Claude Gmail Personal

This app is used by one person only, the owner of the Gmail account it connects to.

  • It accesses Gmail only to read, label, draft and send the owner's own email at the owner's request.
  • Email data stays on the owner's own computer and the AI service the owner uses; it is not sold, shared or used for advertising.
  • No data from other people's Google accounts is collected.
  • The owner can revoke access at any time in their Google Account settings.
